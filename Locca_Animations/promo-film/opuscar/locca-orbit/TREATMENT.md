Style: Satellite Briefing (lemo-opuscar). Client: Locca (B2C campaign). 1080×1920, 16 s, 30 fps, no voice; on-screen readings in TR (EN/RU swap the strings). Storyboard approved by the user on 2026-10-21 (`promo-film/docs/storyboard-uzay-3d.html`, film B).

Five of six changed; the orbit opening and dive stay (they were the storyboard's hook):

## 1. Three structures

1. **The dive and the climb (chosen).** From orbit down to Alanya, a lock-on, the venues bracketed, a range line that pays points at each stop, colour returning only at the café that completes its card, then the climb back to orbit and the title. Locca told as a reconnaissance: find the place, measure the route, read the reward.
2. **Time rings in one frame.** A locked frame over Alanya; each "pass" adds one visit and one reading. Strong, but static for a 9:16 social clip and closer to a data film than a promo.
3. **Two places compared.** Whip pans between two neighbourhoods at one scale, each with its own reward. Needs more imagery and splits attention.

The dive wins: it gives a hook in the first second (the Earth), a clear path to one place, and an ending that returns that place to the planet.

## 2. Logline and arc

Seen from orbit, one small coastal town is where the rewards are.
Setup: the planet, a searching reticle. Turn: the lock-on over Alanya, the route measured stop by stop. Ending: colour returns at the café that completed its card; climb back; the title in the reticle.

## 3. Benchmark

Learn: nested plates with feathered edges and a log-space zoom, the analyst grade, hairlines that are drawn on, one reading at a time in one accent, colour returning where the story turns, the reticle opening into the title.
Don't learn: the demo's place, claim, dates, shots or ending.

## 4. Imagery and truth (FACTS)

- Ground imagery must be licence-clean: NASA Blue Marble (public domain) for orbit; Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A (10 m) for the region and town, credited "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025". No Google, Bing, Esri, Mapbox, Maxar or Planet.
- At 10 m a café's roof can't be seen. The last stretch is the claim layer: a printed Locca map (venues, route) over the real ground. The venues are made up and placed on plausible streets of the old town; the film never claims they are real places. Coordinates shown are Alanya's real centre.
- Readings are Locca rewards, not measurements: shown in the accent colour, never in the white that means "measured".

## 6. Beat sheet

- 0.0 orbit, true colour, Earth limb, ALT 700 km. 0.6 the reticle appears and searches (two moves). 1.8 it settles on the eastern Mediterranean.
- 2.5 the dive begins; the grade fades from colour to analyst grey by 4.0; ALT counts down; the scale bar resizes. 5.2 lock-on brackets snap onto Alanya (ping); label ALANYA · 36.54° K 32.00° D.
- 6.0 a scan bar crosses top → bottom and leaves the printed Locca map: the venues as points with brackets: Kale Sofrası (6.4), Şeker Fırın (6.8), Palm & Bean (7.2).
- 9.0 the range line grows Kale Sofrası → Şeker Fırın → Palm & Bean (ticks every 100 m). Readings land: +12 PUAN (9.6), +40 PUAN (10.4), +45 PUAN (11.2); at Palm & Bean also 7/8 → 8/8 DAMGA (11.6).
- 12.0 a short suck-out; 12.3 colour returns inside Palm & Bean's block only; the big reading 105 PUAN lands at 12.6 (the heaviest hit).
- 14.0 the climb back; the grade returns to colour at orbit. 14.6 the reticle opens into a title frame: "YERELİ KEŞFET. ÖDÜLLERİ KAZAN." + locca logo; the fox (one still pose) at the frame's edge. Hold to 16.0.

## 7. Cue map

No bar grid during the dive (it follows the camera); a 100 BPM pulse from 6.0 (beat 0.6 s).
- Orbit: hum + radio hiss, the lock-on ping motif (FM) at 0.6 and 5.2.
- Dive: a riser 2.5–5.2, ticks that divide as the altitude falls (accelerating), a servo whine on the tilt.
- Claim layer: noise scan at 6.0; a servo click per bracket.
- Range line: a ratchet while it grows; each reading on a pitched hit (gran cassa + glockenspiel for the accent); a pulse bass with 2nd/3rd harmonics under it.
- Turn: 12.0 suck-out (0.3 s, −25 dB), 12.6 the 105 hit: gran cassa + low pulse + the film's one wet timbre (glass / bowed vibraphone).
- Climb: reversed swell, the ping motif once more at 14.6.

## 9. Type

- Reading: Barlow Condensed Light, 110–130 px, accent orange #FF7A2E; label above in IBM Plex Mono.
- Instruments (ALT, scale bar, coordinates, imagery credit): IBM Plex Mono 24–30 px, corners.
- Venue labels: Instrument Sans 34 px on leader lines.
- Title: a serif is the style's title role, but the brand line stays in the brand's voice: a heavy grotesque in the reticle frame, the logo below.
- RU: IBM Plex Mono and Barlow Condensed both have Cyrillic.
