# Minik Alanya gezegeni — treatment

Style: Low-poly Isometric Island (lemo-opuscar). Client: Locca (B2C campaign). 1080×1920, 15 s, 30 fps, wordless until the end title (TR; EN/RU swap only the title). Storyboard approved by the user on 2026-10-21 (`promo-film/docs/storyboard-uzay-3d.html`, film A).

> **Revision after reading DEMO.md (2026-10-21).** The demo is also "an island that grows from one tile, lives through a day, lights its windows at night and ends on a zoom-out". Too close: four of six had to change. What was built instead:
> - **Opening:** a field of dozens of little floating worlds; a log-space zoom dives onto one (Alanya), marked by a soft orange beacon. (The demo opens on one object in an empty sea.)
> - **Structure:** activation, not construction. Alanya is already there but pale ("unvisited"); a Locca pin hops a route tile by tile (the path warms to orange behind it) and each venue it reaches comes alive: colour floods in, sign, awning and umbrellas pop, a points crystal shoots up (+12, +40, +45); Palm & Bean's stamp disc goes 7/8 → 8/8.
> - **Signature shot:** the three crystals rise into orbit and break into a ring of 48 bits around the island: the Loccard; the number counts to 105 and locks.
> - **Camera path:** dive (fh 170 → 22), turntable, a high map-like angle (52°) for the route, then a low angle (20–22°) for the ring.
> - **Score shape:** no silent night or climax; layers are added as venues come alive (bowed vibraphone bed → shaker, bass, cajon → the full D major 9 at the lock).
> - **Ending:** no zoom-out; the ring keeps turning while the title rises and the logo pops. No day/night cycle, no windows lighting up.
> Timings as built: fox 3.0, pin 3.5, legs from 4.5 / 6.25 / 7.75 (a hop every 0.25 s), venues alive at 5.0 / 7.0 / 8.5, stamp 9.0, crystals rise 9.0, ring 10.0, lock (105) 11.0, title 12.2–13.5, logo 13.6.

## 1. Three structures

1. **Growth (chosen).** A world builds itself from one tile: the first tile carries the Locca pin, then the coast, the tower and three venues land, the route and rewards rise, night lights the visited windows, and a zoom-out shows the island as one of many. It is the motto as a process: discover (the world appears) → collect (stamps, points) → win (the ring of 105).
2. **Two islands.** A grey island of separate loyalty cards and a lively Locca island trade pieces across a gap until they join. Clear, but it repeats the "scattered loyalty" clip we just made.
3. **A day in four lights.** One fixed island; morning, noon, dusk and night each add one reward. Calm and pretty, but the rewards would be small events in a big time-lapse, and 15 s is too short for four lights.

Growth wins: every event is a piece landing on a note, which is what the style does best, and it can become a series (each post adds one venue to the same island).

## 2. Logline and arc

A tiny Alanya grows out of the dark, one tile at a time, and every place you visit lights up and pays you back.
Setup: one tile with a pin in the void. Turn: the venues pay out (stamps, points), night falls and the visits light up. Ending: the world among worlds, then the title.

## 3. Benchmark

Learn: arrival curves (rise with overshoot, drop and squash, pop), building as composing (one piece per music slot), light as a clock, pure orthographic zoom for scale.
Don't learn: the demo's story, island shape, shots, props or ending. Our island is Alanya: a coast, a red octagonal tower on a rock (a stylised Kızıl Kule, an old public monument), a harbour; the venues are made up (Palm & Bean café, Şeker Fırın dessert shop, Kale Sofrası restaurant).

## 5. Beat sheet (120 BPM, bar = 2 s, beat = 0.5 s)

- 0.0 void, stars, low drone. 0.5 the first tile rises (splash ring), settles at 1.0 (bell motif). 1.5 the pin pops on top.
- 2.0–5.0 growth on eighths, accelerating: sand and grass tiles (2.0–3.0), the tower (3.0, stone thunk), the harbour pier (3.5), Palm & Bean drops at 4.0, Şeker Fırın 4.25, Kale Sofrası 4.5; trees pop 4.6–5.0.
- 5.0 the fox stands at Palm & Bean (a flat cut-out on a tile, one pose, no walking). 5.5 the orange route draws Kale Sofrası → Şeker Fırın → Palm & Bean. 6.0, 6.5, 7.0 at each venue a points crystal rises (+12, +40, +45 shown small on the crystal); 7.5 a stamp disc rises at Palm & Bean: 7/8 → 8/8.
- 8.0 dusk → 9.0 night. 8.5, 9.0, 9.5 the three venues' windows light (whump + hum). 10.0 the crystals join an orbit ring; the ring's number counts to 105 and lands at 10.5.
- 11.0 the zoom-out begins; other small islands appear (one more orange glow far away) by 12.5.
- 13.0 title letters rise: "YERELİ KEŞFET." 13.0–13.6, "ÖDÜLLERİ KAZAN." 13.6–14.2, the locca logo 14.2. Hold to 15.0.

## 6. Cue map

120 BPM, 7.5 bars, D major pentatonic (no fourths or sevenths, so any landing order stays consonant).
- Bar 1 (0–2): drone (bowed vibraphone, low), the bell motif at 1.0 (hand chimes D5).
- Bars 2–3 (2–5): every landing is a note: kalimba for tiles, woodblock for the pier, marimba-low "thunk" for the tower, vibraphone for the venues; light shaker from 3.0.
- Bar 4 (5–8): a gentle ostinato (kalimba eighths), crystals on glass/glockenspiel, the stamp on a woodblock + bell.
- Bar 5 (8–11): night: the ostinato thins to one voice; each lit window is a vibraphone note with a low hum; 10.5 (105) a full D-major-9 chord on vibraphone + glockenspiel.
- Bar 6 (11–13): the motif bell once more, far (reverb up).
- Bars 7–7.5 (13–15): each title letter a soft kalimba note; the logo on the bell; release.

## Revision 2 (user feedback, 2026-10-21)

- One reward per venue: a venue pays points OR a stamp, never both. Kale Sofrası +45 points, Şeker Fırın +60 points (= 105), Palm & Bean a stamp only (its own card, 7/8 → 8/8).
- The fox sticker is replaced by a low-poly fox built from primitives in the world's own flat shading. It is the traveller: it pops up at the start facing us, then hops the route one tile per eighth note and stops beside each venue (half turned to us), and jumps when the ring locks. Trees and houses are cleared from the tiles it crosses.
- The 105 no longer floats at the top of the frame: it sits on the ring's front right, and the Palm & Bean stamp (8/8) moves to the ring's front left, so the payoff shows both reward types.
- Revision 3: the dive is one continuous log-space zoom (it used to end at fh 17 and restart at 22, a visible jump); the far islands shrink away instead of popping. Palm & Bean's stamp card is a low-poly 3D object in the scene's own style (bevelled slab, badge, printed name, eight plain octagonal stamps without ticks): it rises over the café showing 7/8, the 8th stamp lands 1 s later, then it floats up above the ring, outside the orbit, and stays to the end.
- Revision 4: the points are low-poly too and float together with the stamp: a dark LOCCARD slab (printed LOCCARD / PUAN, a spinning crystal, the total as extruded 3D digits counting 0 → 105) on the left, Palm & Bean's stamp card on the right, side by side above the ring.
